Solara Heat — an open-world crime game in the fictional city of Port Solara. Walk and drive a road-grid city full of traffic and pedestrians, steal cars, earn a wanted level and outrun the Solara PD, spend cash at Pay 'n' Spray, and run four story missions from the yellow S marker at the Safehouse. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or just open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests: plain static files.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrows** — walk / drive (W accelerates, S brakes/reverses)
- **E** — enter / exit a car (steal it if someone is driving), start a mission at the S marker
- **Space** — punch on foot, handbrake while driving
- **Shift** — sprint on foot
- **Esc / P** — pause
- **M** — mute

## Missions

1. **Hot Wheels** — steal a car and drive it to the Docks ($150)
2. **Package Run** — deliver 3 packages across the city in 2:30 ($300)
3. **Heat Wave** — grab the cash at Solara Bank, then lose the cops and reach the Safehouse ($500)
4. **The Big Score** — crack the bank vault while the whole PD swarms, then escape ($1,000)

After all four: free roam as the king of Port Solara.
